Cabinet

Keep track of what you own.

Shoes, bags, tumblers — whatever fills your cupboards. Photograph it, filter it, and know how old everything is. Yours alone: nobody else can see what you put in.

Try it in the browser

What it does

One page, no account, no subscription. It installs to your home screen and works without a signal.

Drawers for anything

Start with shoes, bags and tumblers, or make your own and choose exactly which details each one tracks.

Filters that narrow

Pick a brand and every other filter shrinks to what that brand actually has. Like a good online shop.

Several photos each

Shoot straight from your phone camera. Photos are compressed on the way in so you don't fill your storage.

How old, and how long

Record when you bought something and see its age, alongside how long that kind of thing usually lasts.

A four-digit lock

Keeps it private on a shared phone. More than one person can have their own code and their own things.

Works offline

Install it once and it opens with no connection at all — on a plane, in a basement, anywhere.

Where your things are kept

You choose the first time you open it, and you can change your mind later.

Option one

Just on this device

Everything stays in the browser on the device you're using. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, nothing needs signing up for, and it works with no connection. The trade-off is that it stays on that one device.

Option two

In your own Google account

Create a free Firebase project under your own Gmail — about five minutes — and the app keeps everything there instead. It then syncs across your phone, tablet and computer, and you can share the cabinet with anyone you choose by giving them the same details. It is your project on your account: the person who gave you this app cannot see any of it.

Downloading gives you a single .html file. Open it in any browser — that's the whole app, nothing to install.
